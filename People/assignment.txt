The purpose of this assignment is to explore, understand, and communicate meaningful insights about a real-world dataset. You will conduct an exploratory data analysis (EDA) of the People dataset using Python and standard data science libraries.



This assignment is not primarily about building predictive models. While you may build predictive models as part of your exploration, the goal is to understand relationships in the data.



Throughout your analysis, focus on asking and answering interesting questions about the dataset. Strive to uncover meaningful real-world patterns and explain why they may exist.



Think of yourself as a data analyst trying to understand patterns in census data rather than a machine learning engineer trying to maximize prediction performance.



Dataset
Use the People dataset.

people_data.arff


PeopleDataDocumentation.txt


Before conducting any analysis, spend time becoming familiar with the meaning of the variables. The PeopleDataDocumentation should be helpful.



WARNING: Because this is a public dataset, you could find many analyses of this dataset online or analyze the data with AI. Referencing these analyses or using AI to generate an analysis of the dataset completely defeats the purpose of the assignment. Treat this assignment as if existing analysis of the dataset did not exist.



Required Areas of Exploration
Your analysis should include evidence of exploration at three different levels.

1. Exploring Individual Features
Investigate variables one at a time. Examples include:

Descriptive statistics
Distributions
Histograms
Boxplots
Counts and frequencies
Missing values
Outlier identification
Variability and spread


Develop an understanding of individual variables before examining relationships between them.



2. Applying Apriori to Mine Association Rules

Students are required to apply the Apriori algorithm, using the python code below. Students must translate the dataframe into a format required by the implementation. The unit tests are also attached for reference.



apriori_from_scratch.py


test_apriori_from_scratch.py


The discovered association rules must explicitly guide further analysis of the dataset. Document how the association rules inform exploring relationships between pairs of features.



3. Exploring Relationships Between Pairs of Features
Investigate patterns involving two variables at a time. Examples include:

Correlation analysis
Scatterplots and other visualizations
Cross-tabulations
Grouped summary statistics
Relationships between categorical and numerical features
Focus on discovering and explaining meaningful patterns. Note: pretty graphs are not always meaningful.



4. Optional Analysis: Feel free to do additional exploration or analysis, such as predicting the target class.

Communicating Your Findings
Your final submission should communicate a coherent story about the dataset. Rather than presenting a collection of unrelated charts and statistics, organize your work around questions, observations, and conclusions. For every important finding:

Describe what you observed.
Provide evidence supporting the observation.
Explain why the finding may be meaningful in a real-world context.
The strongest submissions will demonstrate curiosity about the data and pursue interesting avenues of investigation beyond the most obvious relationships.
